CORE FEATURES

Covariance & Variable Relationships

Covariance indicates how two continuous random variables change together.

Mathematical Definition of Covariance
Cov(X, Y) = E[(X - E[X])(Y - E[Y])] = E[XY] - E[X]E[Y]

Interpretation: Positive covariance indicates a positive statistical association where variables tend to vary together. Negative covariance indicates an inverse association. Covariance reflects joint variability and does not imply causation.

How Credit Risk is Calculated

Step-by-step probabilistic decision pipeline implementing Bayesian classification principles.

01

Financial Inputs

User inputs 6 continuous variables: Income, Score, Loan Amount, Years, DTI, Age.

→
02

Standard Scaling

Z-Score normalization (z = (x - μ) / σ) to standardize disparate financial magnitudes.

→
03

Gaussian Likelihood

Compute conditional probability density P(xᵢ | Y = c) via Gaussian bell curve formula.

→
04

Conditional Independence

Multiply likelihoods: P(X | Y = c) = ∏ P(xᵢ | Y = c) under the naive assumption.

→
05

Bayes' Posterior & Risk

Apply Bayes' theorem to output exact P(Default | X) and categorize into Low/Medium/High.

Equation 1

Bayes' Theorem

P(Y = c | X) = [P(X | Y = c) × P(Y = c)] / P(X)

Estimates posterior belief of loan default given observed borrower evidence. P(Y) represents the prior historical default rate (47.4%).

Equation 2

Gaussian Probability Density

P(xᵢ | Y=c) = (1 / √(2πσ²)) exp(-(xᵢ - μ)² / (2σ²))

Because loan features are continuous, each feature is modeled as a normal distribution parameterized by mean (μ) and variance (σ²) per class.

Core Assumption

Class-Conditional Independence

P(x₁, x₂, ..., x₆ | Y) = ∏ᵢ₌₁⁶ P(xᵢ | Y)

Assumes all input features are independent given the loan outcome. This dramatic simplification makes high-dimensional Bayesian inference computationally tractable.

Decision Boundary

MAP Decision & Thresholds

Risk = f(P(Default | X))

Probabilities are mapped to decision tiers: <40% (Low Risk), 40%–69.9% (Medium Risk), and ≥70% (High Risk Default Alert).
